Professional Booking Form update — 10 August 2026

- Four-step booking flow: Details, Service, Appointment, Confirm
- New/Returning client pill buttons
- Service selection cards instead of a long dropdown
- Add-ons only shown after a service is selected; covered add-on only appears when compatible
- Next available appointment retained

- Public availability notes only appear when a relevant date is tapped

- Existing Supabase pending-booking save, Realtime availability and WhatsApp message retained
