CLOSING-TIME / DURATION FIX
===========================

This correction removes the accidental fixed 60-minute admin default and prevents client appointments from running past closing time.

Changes:
- Admin confirmed-booking duration now defaults to 30 minutes instead of 60 minutes.
- 60, 90 and 120 minute durations are still available when they are actually needed.
- Public website availability is calculated from the selected service duration (30 or 60 minutes).
- A client appointment is only offered when the SERVICE itself finishes on or before closing time.
- The private buffer still reserves time after an appointment, but it does not give the client extra appointment time and may extend after closing.
- Admin confirmation also refuses any client appointment whose actual end time is after closing.
- Manual time-block end now defaults to 30 minutes after the chosen start rather than 60 minutes.
- Schedule App cache bumped to v7 so installed PWAs refresh the corrected JavaScript.

Examples with a 17:00 closing time:
- 30-minute service at 16:30 -> allowed (finishes 17:00).
- 60-minute service at 16:00 -> allowed (finishes 17:00).
- 30-minute service at 16:45 -> not offered by the current slot pattern.
- 60-minute service at 16:15 -> NOT allowed (would finish 17:15).

No new Supabase SQL migration is required for this correction.
