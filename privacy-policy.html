Privacy Policy

This policy explains how booking and contact information may be handled when you communicate with Massage by Ashleigh.

Information You Provide

Information may include your name, mobile number, preferred appointment details, payment confirmation and any information you choose to share for booking or safety purposes.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange and manage appointments, confirm payments, maintain safety and communicate changes relating to a booking. When you submit the website booking form, the request is stored as a Pending booking in Ashleigh's secure booking database before you continue to WhatsApp.

Storage, Sharing & Confidentiality

Booking records are stored in Ashleigh's appointment-management database and are accessible only to Ashleigh. The public calendar receives only sanitised availability information such as dates and occupied time ranges; client names, phone numbers and private booking notes are not published. Personal information is treated discreetly and is not sold.

Retention & Requests

Booking information may be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for administration, safety or legal obligations. Requests about personal information can be made through the website WhatsApp contact.

External Links

The website may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp and OnlyFans. Their own privacy terms apply when those services are used.
